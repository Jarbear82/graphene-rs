### 1. Simple algorithm for computing a maximal planar subgraph
**(Figure 2.15, Section 2.5.2)**

```
Require: graph G = (V, E)
Ensure: maximal planar subgraph P of G

P := a spanning tree of G
F := E \ E(P)
for all e ∈ F do
    if P ∪ e is planar then
        P := P ∪ e
    end if
end for
```
